# AI use log

JCQ's *AI Use in Assessments* (in force from 30 April 2025) says:

- Any AI use must be acknowledged: name the tool (for example "ChatGPT, GPT-5") and the date of each use.
- Keep a non-editable copy (screenshot or PDF) of the prompts and the AI's answers, with a short note on how you used
  them, and hand it in with the work.
- Work the AI produced is not credited as yours, even when you acknowledge it. Marks come only from what you did.
- Ask your teacher what the school allows before using AI on any assessed part.
