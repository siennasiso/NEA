# PawMatch tester script (facilitator's copy)

**App:** https://shaans-mac-mini.tail100d11.ts.net/pawmatch · **Time:** about 3 minutes per tester · **You need:** this sheet, one feedback form per tester, a phone stopwatch.

## Before each tester (30 s)
1. Open the app on the device the tester will use (phone or laptop). Make sure nobody is signed in.
2. Write the tester number (Tester 1 to 5), age band, device and pet experience at the top of their form.
3. Read aloud: *"I'm testing the website, not you. Please think aloud as you go. I can't help, so if you get stuck, just say so."*

## The five tasks
Read each task aloud exactly as written. Start the stopwatch when you finish reading, and stop it when the success condition is met or the tester gives up.

| Task | Read aloud | Success when |
|---|---|---|
| U1 | "Create an account for yourself and sign in." | The home page shows their name. Every error message they met was understood without your help. |
| U2 | "Fill in the questionnaire to find animals that suit your home." | The matches page appears. Target: under 3 minutes. |
| U3 | "Find your top match. Tell me why it suits you, and what any warning means." | They explain the match and the warning correctly in their own words. |
| U4 | (Staff tester only; you sign in with the staff demo account first.) "Add a new animal, change its age, then delete it." | The animal appears, the change is shown, then it is gone. |
| U5 | "Browse the animals, open one animal's profile, then go back to the list." | They are back on the list with the same filters. |

## What to watch for (write it in the comment box)
- **Hesitation:** a pause of more than 5 seconds, or "where do I…?"
- **Wrong taps:** pressing the wrong button or going back. Count them as **errors**.
- **Error messages:** which one appeared, and did they understand it?
- **Reading aloud:** words they misread or asked about (for example "welfare need").
- **Device:** anything too small to tap, or text they had to zoom to read.

## Facilitator's rules
- **Never help, point or hint.** If they ask, say "What would you try?" After 2 minutes stuck, mark the task **not done** and move on.
- Note the **time in seconds**, the **number of errors** and **done Y/N** straight away for each task.
- At the end, the tester rates each task 1 to 10 (1 = very easy, 10 = very hard) and fills in the ten SUS statements alone.
- Delete nothing except the animal added in U4. Test accounts can stay.
